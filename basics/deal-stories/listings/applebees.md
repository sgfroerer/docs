<summary>Dine Brands Global - Completes Debt Refinancing</summary>

{% hint style="info" %}
Dine Brands Global, Inc. (fka DineEquity, Inc.)\
`Credit Rating: D1`
{% endhint %}

* Dine Brands Global, Inc. announced the completion of a refinancing of its senior secured notes and variable funding senior notes. The Company issued $600 million of 6.72% Series 2025-1 Class A-2 senior secured notes. The 6.72% notes require principal and interest payments on a quarterly basis, though the principal payments may be suspended if the Company's leverage ratio is less than or equal to 5.25x. The notes have an expected term of five years, though the legal final maturity is in June 2055.
* The Company also entered into a new revolving financing facility consisting of $325 million of Series 2025-1 Class A-1 variable funding senior notes, to replace its previous $325 million revolving financing facility (Series 2022-1 Class A-1 variable funding senior notes). Borrowings bear interest at term SOFR plus 2.5%. The facility is expected to be repaid in full by June 2030, subject to two one-year extensions at the option of the Company.
* Net proceeds from the new notes will be used to repay all $594 million in outstanding Series 2019-1 Class A-2-II senior secured notes due June 2026, and $100 million of outstanding borrowings under the Series 2022-1 Class A-1 variable funding senior notes.
