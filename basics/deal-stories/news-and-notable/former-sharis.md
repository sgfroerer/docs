# Former Shari’s

Shari’s Cafe & Pies



Founded in Hermiston in 1978, Shari’s was a Pacific Northwest late-night staple before the chain [shuttered all its remaining Oregon locations](https://www.oregonlive.com/business/2024/10/struggling-sharis-cafe-pies-closes-additional-locations.html) last fall — the last of the bunch being its restaurant near Portland International Airport.

***



In April, Shake Shake opened its first Vancouver location at the site of one of the former pie cafes, and in February In-N-Out filed plans to debut at another ex-Shari’s lot in Gresham.



Elmer’s currently operates nine restaurants in the Portland-area — including one at 10001 N.E. Sandy Blvd., less than a mile from the forthcoming Airport Way location.
