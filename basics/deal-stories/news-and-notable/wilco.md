<summary>A Northwest Company Works to Expand into Aloha</summary>

Behind the scenes of the building vacant since Basics moved out in January of 2024, a new tenant works to join the Aloha Community and it’s not another grocery store. On April 4, a virtual public forum was held about Baysinger Partners Architecture’s plans for several redesigns on the outside of the building at Kinnaman and Farmington for their new client.

\
The presenters said that at this time, they cannot say the specific company that will be the new tenant. Jennifer Rinkus, the project planner from Baysinger, did state that it is a Northwest local farm store. Fellow presenter Jerad Lillegard, whose public LinkedIn page says he is the Director of Construction and Facilities at Wilco, said that the new tenant is excited to be welcomed into the area and to be a part of our community.



During the public forum, they presented several slides showing plans for changes to accommodate the new store. Changes include repurposing several parking spots and existing awnings for outdoor sales areas, remodeling the left side of the building for a customer drive-thru loading area and a loading door. An expanded greenhouse is also planned as well as a hay sales area.

The project is currently working on permitting for the changes and design plans for the interior. It may take a few months to get the permits with Washington County. Rinkus says that the aim will be for the store to open in the first quarter.

> Rinkus says that the aim will be for the store to open in the first quarter.

\
When asked how the new store might affect the Aloha Community Farmer’s market that meets at the Farmington Market Place parking lot, Lillegard said that he doesn’t believe there will be any impact on the Farmer’s Market. Rinkus agreed that the work and changes to the building shouldn’t interfere with the farmer’s market location. Aloha Community Farmer’s Market Manager, Kyra Ramsa&#x79;**,** told The Aloha Free Press they “welcome the possibility of the new tenant joining our marketplace,” and look forward to working with their new neighbor.
